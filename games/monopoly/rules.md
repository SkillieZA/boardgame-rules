# Monopoly — Quick Guide

Travel around the board buying property, collecting rent, and making deals. The aim is to remain financially solvent after every rival has gone bankrupt.

## Set up

- Choose a banker to manage money, deeds, buildings, and auctions.
- Shuffle the card decks and place each player token on the starting space.
- Give each player the starting money specified by your edition.

## On your turn

1. Roll both dice and move clockwise by the total.
2. Resolve the space you land on: buy, pay rent or tax, draw a card, or follow its instruction.
3. Rolling doubles normally grants another roll. Repeated doubles may send you to jail.

## Property and auctions

- You may buy an unowned property you land on for its listed price.
- If you decline, the banker auctions it to all players. The landing player may also bid.
- Complete colour groups allow houses and, later, hotels to be built.
- Build evenly across properties in the same colour group.

## Trading and money

- Players may trade property, money, and eligible cards by mutual agreement.
- Mortgage property or sell buildings when you need funds.
- A player who cannot settle a debt after raising funds is bankrupt and leaves the game.

## Winning

The last player who has not gone bankrupt wins. For a shorter session, agree on a time limit and compare total assets when time ends.

> This is a sample quick guide. Starting cash, special spaces, and optional short-game rules vary by edition; use your included rulebook for final rulings.
