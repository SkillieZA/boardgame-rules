# Wingspan — Quick Guide

A bird-collection engine-building game played over four rounds. Build habitats, activate bird powers, and finish with the most points.

## Set up

- Give each player a habitat mat, action cubes, initial bird cards, and food tokens.
- Prepare the face-up bird-card tray, bonus cards, goals, eggs, and food supply.
- Roll all five food dice and place their results in the birdfeeder.
- Choose a first player and complete the starting-card and food choices described by your edition.

## Choose one action

1. Play a bird by paying its food and egg costs and placing it in a matching habitat.
2. Gain food from the forest row, taking available dice from the birdfeeder.
3. Lay eggs using the grassland row, respecting each bird's egg capacity.
4. Draw bird cards using the wetland row.

When using a habitat action, resolve the printed action space and then activate eligible brown powers from right to left.

## Using the birdfeeder dice

- Roll all five dice when filling an empty birdfeeder.
- Each die shows fish, fruit, invertebrate, rodent, seed, or a choice of invertebrate or seed.
- When taking food, move the chosen die out of the available tray by dragging it right into the set-aside tray.
- If all dice remaining in the birdfeeder show the same face and you are about to gain food, you may reroll all five dice under the core rules.

## Round end and scoring

At the end of each round, resolve the current goal and place one action cube on the goal board. This leaves every player with one fewer turn in the next round.

After round four, total points from birds, bonus cards, end-of-round goals, eggs, cached food, and tucked cards. The highest score wins.

> Expansions add food types, boards, powers, and scoring rules. This guide describes the base game; use the rulebooks supplied with your game for final rulings.
