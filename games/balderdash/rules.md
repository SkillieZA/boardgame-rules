# Balderdash — Quick Guide

A bluffing game where everyone invents believable answers to obscure questions, then tries to identify the real answer among the fakes.

## Set up

- Give every player several answer slips and a pencil.
- Place all playing pieces at the start of the board, if your edition includes one.
- Choose the first reader, often called the Dasher.
- Set the writing timer to 60 seconds.

## Play a round

1. The Dasher draws a card, announces the category, and reads the prompt aloud without revealing the answer.
2. Start the timer. Every other player secretly writes a convincing invented answer and signs or marks the slip so the Dasher can identify its author.
3. The Dasher writes the real answer on another slip, then collects and discreetly checks all submissions.
4. If a player submitted the real or essentially correct answer, set that slip aside and award the result specified by your edition.
5. The Dasher shuffles the remaining slips with the real answer and reads every answer aloud in a neutral voice.
6. Going clockwise, each player chooses the answer they believe is correct. Players may not vote for their own answer.
7. Reveal the truth, score the round, and move any playing pieces.
8. Pass the Dasher role clockwise and begin the next round.

## Writing good bluffs

- Match the style, length, and level of detail you expect from the real answer.
- Write clearly enough for the Dasher to read your answer without giving away who wrote it.
- Keep your answer plausible; confidence is often more convincing than comedy.
- Do not consult a dictionary, phone, or outside source unless everyone agrees to allow it.

## Scoring

Common editions award points for choosing the real answer and for every opponent fooled by your invented answer. The Dasher may also score when nobody finds the truth, while a player who already knows the answer may receive a separate reward.

> Exact categories, movement, challenges, and point values differ between editions. Use the rulebook supplied with your game for scoring and disputed answers.

## Winning

Continue until a player reaches the finish space or the winning score specified by your edition. If several players reach it in the same round, use your edition's tie-break rule or share the victory.
