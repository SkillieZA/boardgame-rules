# Risk — Quick Guide

A strategy game of reinforcement, territorial expansion, and calculated battles. Complete the objective specified by your edition before your opponents do.

## Set up

- Choose armies and distribute territories and starting troops as directed by your edition.
- Place remaining troops into territories you control.
- Shuffle the territory cards and prepare any objective cards used by your edition.
- Choose the first player.

## On your turn

1. Reinforce your territories using the number of troops you are entitled to receive.
2. Attack adjacent enemy territories as often as you choose, or skip attacking.
3. After attacking, make one fortification move between connected territories you control, if your edition allows it.
4. Draw a territory card when you qualify, then pass play clockwise.

## Resolving a battle

- The attacker chooses one to three red dice and must leave at least one troop behind.
- The defender chooses one or two white dice, limited by the number of defending troops.
- Roll the chosen dice and arrange each side's results from highest to lowest.
- Compare the highest pair, then the second-highest pair if both sides rolled at least two dice.
- The lower result loses one troop. The defender wins ties.

> In the app, tap dice to include them in a roll. Drag unused dice right into the set-aside tray and drag them left to return them.

## Capturing and winning

When the final defending troop is removed, the attacker moves troops into the captured territory. Continue until a player completes the objective or victory condition specified by the edition being played.

> Card trading, setup, objectives, and special rules vary considerably between editions. Use the rulebook supplied with your game for final rulings.
