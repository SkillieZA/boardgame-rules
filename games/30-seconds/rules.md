# 30 Seconds — Quick Guide

A rapid team game where one player describes names or topics while teammates race to identify them before time expires.

## Set up

- Divide everyone into teams and choose a team marker.
- Put the prompt cards within reach of the active describer.
- Choose a starting team and set the timer to 30 seconds.

## On your turn

1. Start the timer when the describer sees the prompts.
2. Describe as many prompts as possible in any order.
3. Teammates may make unlimited guesses until time expires.
4. Count the correctly guessed prompts and update the team's score or board position.

## Clue guidelines

- Do not say the answer or a direct part of it.
- Do not spell it, give its first letter, or say what it rhymes with.
- Use descriptions, associations, people, places, and events instead.
- Skip a difficult prompt and return to it if time remains.

## Winning

Continue taking turns by team. The first team to reach the agreed target or finish space wins.

> This is a sample quick guide. Card restrictions, scoring, and board movement can differ between editions, so use the rulebook supplied with your game for final rulings.
